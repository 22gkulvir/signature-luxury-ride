Operating Authority

Dallas City Code Ch. 47A-2.1.1  |  TxDMV  |  FMCSA

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. is a licensed transportation-for-hire operating authority in the City of Dallas, Texas. We hold all required permits to operate pre-arranged black car and luxury SUV transportation services across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex.

Company: Signature Luxury Ride Inc.
Service Area: Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex, Texas
Base of Operations: Garland, TX
Service Type: Pre-arranged luxury transportation for hire (non-hailable)

All vehicles are properly registered, inspected, and maintained in compliance with City of Dallas and State of Texas requirements. Operating authority permits are available for inspection upon request.

2

Rates & Fares

Dallas City Code SEC. 47A-2.1.7 & SEC. 47A-2.4.8

In accordance with Dallas City Code SEC. 47A-2.4.8, rates and fares for all transportation-for-hire services are made available on this publicly remotely accessible data site.

All fares are pre-arranged and quoted at the time of booking — no meters, no hidden fees. Service types include airport transfers, point-to-point transfers, hourly chauffeur, corporate travel, special events, and group transportation. Applicable tolls and fees are passed through at cost. A confirmed quote is provided to every passenger prior to the trip.

For current pricing, please contact us directly. Rates are subject to change and are available upon request.

Insurance Coverage

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. maintains commercial automobile liability insurance that meets or exceeds all minimum requirements under City of Dallas ordinances, Texas state law, and applicable Federal regulations. Zero Tolerance — Drugs & Alcohol

Dallas City Code SEC. 47A-2.2.12  |  Texas Occupations Code Ch. 2402

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. maintains a strict Zero Tolerance Policy. No driver may operate a vehicle while under the influence of alcohol, controlled substances, or any impairing drugs while on duty or transporting passengers.

To Report a Concern: Call us immediately at Loading… or contact Dallas Police at 911 in an emergency.

Driver Standards & Qualifications

Dallas City Code SEC. 47A-2.2.2 & SEC. 47A-2.2.12

All drivers meet or exceed the qualifications required under City of Dallas Chapter 47A, including a valid Texas driver's license, criminal background check, driving history review, and completion of required safety training. Driver and vehicle permits are subject to periodic renewal and inspection as required by the City of Dallas.

Non-Discrimination Policy

Dallas City Code Ch. 47A  |  49 CFR Part 374  |  Civil Rights Act

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. does not discriminate and will not refuse service to any person on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local law. Service animals are permitted in all vehicles at no additional charge.

7

No Smoking Policy

49 CFR Part 374.301  |  Dallas City Code Ch. 47A

Smoking and the use of tobacco or electronic cigarette products is strictly prohibited in all vehicles at all times, as required under 49 CFR §374.301 and City of Dallas ordinances.

8

Accessibility (ADA Compliance)

Americans with Disabilities Act  |  49 CFR Part 37

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. is committed to serving all passengers. We will not refuse service on the basis of disability. Service animals are permitted in all vehicles at no additional charge. Passengers requiring accommodations are encouraged to note their needs at the time of booking.

Privacy & Data Policy

Texas Data Privacy & Security Act (TDPSA)  |  Texas Bus. & Com. Code Ch. 541

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. collects only the information necessary to fulfill your reservation. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. Texas residents have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act. Contact us to exercise these rights.
