Insurance Coverage

Dallas City Code Ch. 47A  |  Texas Insurance Code Ch. 1954  |  FMCSA 49 CFR Part 387

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. maintains commercial automobile liability insurance that meets or exceeds all minimum requirements under City of Dallas ordinances, Texas state law, and applicable Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA) regulations.

  • Commercial Liability Insurance: Minimum $1,000,000 per occurrence, as required for for-hire passenger vehicle operations in the City of Dallas.
  • Coverage Period: Insurance is active at all times during vehicle operation, including during the period from dispatch through passenger drop-off.
  • Interstate Operations: Where rides cross state lines, coverage satisfies FMCSA minimum financial responsibility requirements (49 CFR §387.33).

Proof of insurance is available upon request and on file with the City of Dallas Code Compliance Department. All insurance certificates are renewed prior to expiration and maintained in each vehicle.

Zero Tolerance — Drugs & Alcohol

Dallas City Code SEC. 47A-2.2.12  |  Texas Occupations Code Ch. 2402

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. maintains a strict Zero Tolerance Policy prohibiting any driver from operating a vehicle while under the influence of, or in possession of, any amount of alcohol, controlled substances, or impairing drugs — whether prescribed or otherwise — while on duty, dispatched, or transporting passengers.

  • No driver may consume alcohol or use intoxicating substances before or during any shift.
  • Drivers are subject to pre-employment background checks and ongoing monitoring.
  • Any passenger who suspects a driver may be impaired should immediately ask to end the trip and contact us and local law enforcement.
  • Violations result in immediate termination and reporting to relevant authorities.

To Report a Concern: Call us immediately at Loading… or contact Dallas Police at 911 in an emergency.

Driver Standards & Qualifications

Dallas City Code SEC. 47A-2.2.2 & SEC. 47A-2.2.12

All Signature Luxury Ride drivers meet or exceed the qualifications required under City of Dallas Chapter 47A, including:

  • Valid Texas driver's license in good standing
  • Minimum age of 18 years
  • Comprehensive criminal background check (state and federal)
  • Driving history review — no major violations within the past 3 years
  • Proof of U.S. work authorization
  • Completion of company safety and service training
  • Professional appearance and conduct standards

Driver permits and vehicle permits are subject to periodic renewal and inspection as required by the City of Dallas.

Non-Discrimination Policy

Dallas City Code Ch. 47A  |  49 CFR Part 374  |  Civil Rights Act

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. does not discriminate and will not refuse service to any person on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic protected by federal, state, or local law.

  • Service is available to all passengers regardless of origin, destination, or personal characteristics.
  • Drivers may not refuse a trip based on any protected characteristic.
  • Service animals are welcome in all vehicles at no additional charge, in compliance with the Americans with Disabilities Act.
  • Any act of discrimination by a driver is grounds for immediate termination.

To report a discrimination concern, contact us directly or file a complaint with the City of Dallas Code Compliance Department.

No Smoking Policy

49 CFR Part 374.301  |  Dallas City Code Ch. 47A

Smoking, vaping, and the use of any tobacco or electronic cigarette products is strictly prohibited in all Signature Luxury Ride vehicles at all times — by both drivers and passengers. This policy applies to all substances including tobacco, cannabis, and e-cigarettes.

Passengers who violate this policy may be asked to exit the vehicle and may be charged a cleaning fee. This requirement is mandated under 49 CFR §374.301 (Federal Smoking Prohibition) and City of Dallas ordinances.

Accessibility (ADA Compliance)

Americans with Disabilities Act  |  49 CFR Part 37

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. is committed to serving all passengers, including those with disabilities, to the greatest extent possible with our current fleet.

  • Service Animals: Service animals are always permitted in all vehicles at no additional charge.
  • Mobility Assistance: Drivers will assist passengers in boarding and exiting vehicles upon request.
  • Luggage & Equipment: Drivers will assist with loading and unloading mobility aids, luggage, and equipment.
  • No Refusal: We will not refuse service to a passenger solely on the basis of a disability.

Passengers requiring specific accessibility accommodations are encouraged to note their needs at the time of booking so we may best prepare for their ride.

Privacy & Data Policy

Texas Data Privacy & Security Act (TDPSA)  |  Texas Bus. & Com. Code Ch. 541

Signature Luxury Ride Inc. collects only the information necessary to fulfill your reservation and communicate with you regarding your ride. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

  • Information Collected: Name, phone number, email address, pickup/drop-off location, and trip details provided at booking.
  • How It's Used: Solely to confirm, dispatch, and complete your reservation, and to communicate service-related updates.
  • Retention: Booking records are retained as required by applicable law and for legitimate business purposes.
  • Third Parties: We use EmailJS solely for form delivery. No personal data is stored or processed by third parties for advertising.
  • Your Rights: Under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, Texas residents have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold. Contact us to exercise these rights.

This website does not use tracking cookies for advertising. Feedback submitted through this website is stored locally on your device and is not shared publicly without your consent.
